## Production solver

The architectural engine is `packages/generator/src/blueprint`, not the older planar `generatePlan` implementation. `input.ts` exports `BLUEPRINT_VERSION = precision-blueprint-v1`, `BlueprintRequirementsSchema` and `normalizeBlueprintRequirements`. `generate.ts:generateBlueprint` orchestrates construction and validates the winner. Defaults are 32 candidates and 20,000 iterations; public maxima are 128 and 20,000. Rooms are capped at 24, relationships at 48, dimensions at 60,000 mm, floors at two.

* `geometry.ts:buildConstraints` accepts only four-vertex axis-aligned rectangles for site and optional buildable boundary. It intersects rectangular setbacks/buildable extents and fills that permitted rectangle. `rectangular` rejects other geometry. It never decomposes an L-shape.
* `buildConstraints` assigns explicit levels first, then balances automatic room counts. Hard relationships do not influence floor assignment.
* `layout.ts:candidateSchedule` shuffles room order in eight seeded rounds, tries z/x corridor orientations, and balanced through unbalanced bank splits. Two floors force all rooms into one bank beside a narrow full-depth stair core and corridor.
* `allocateCandidate` reserves the corridor and wall faces, computes clear-room minimums (including area and doorway reserve), then uses `geometry.ts:reconcilePartition` to distribute remaining integer millimetres equally, earliest remainder first. Two-floor stair clearance requires run plus two landings.
* `scoreCandidate` rejects hard relationships without exact same-level shared box boundaries. Adjacent needs positive contact; connected/passage reserves door width, jambs and wall allowance. Soft relationship misses cost 1000; preferred dimension errors cost ceil(mm/100); front/rear misses cost 100. Front means world min-z regardless of north or actual entrance side.
* `entities.ts:constructWalls` groups and splits orthogonal space boundaries at every junction. `constructModel` builds explicit walls, hosted doors/passages/windows, levels, floor/roof slabs and straight stairs. Each requested room has its own corridor door. The entrance is the corridor's low-coordinate end. No canonical primary-entry designation exists: the API selects exterior ground-storey doors.
* `constructModel` inserts requested connected doors and passage openings on shared walls. Open kitchen remains separate rooms with a normal-width direct door; Phase 18 warns about this.
* Windows use the first exterior host with sufficient jamb clearance and no other opening. There is no exposure-based host ranking.
* The stair is fixed at the front of the reserved side core, in +x or +z, with exact reconciled riser heights, two landings and an upper slab void. `landingDoorFits` and `validation.ts:analyzeCirculation` check corresponding landing access.
* `validation.ts:validateBlueprint` applies both V2 schemas, clear minima/count/level checks, physical wall containment, wall/clear-room non-overlap, stair/void checks and entrance reachability. `dimensions.ts:constructDimensions` creates exact controlling chains and entity dimensions.
* `random.ts:seededRandom` and `shuffled` provide the seed-derived sequence. V1 prefixes its seed with its own engine version. There is no wall-clock cutoff; equal scores retain the first candidate. Failures are typed `BlueprintError`, but complete family exhaustion can say `IMPOSSIBLE_LAYOUT`; it is not a proof over all architectures.

## Canonical model, independent QA and downstream contracts

`packages/shared/src/building-v2/geometry.ts` already supports bounded simple polygons using BigInt predicates, `ringContains`, and positive-area `ringsOverlap`. Rings are **implicitly closed** ordered vertices: a duplicate closing vertex is invalid. Preserve this historical convention, including the closing-edge validation; do not introduce a GeoJSON ring convention silently. Building Model V2 supports polygonal levels/spaces/plates, straight hosted walls/openings and straight stairs. It has no parking entity or explicit primary-entry field. Additions must be optional so old model bodies and hashes remain unchanged.

`packages/blueprint-qa/src/audit.ts:auditBlueprint` consumes `BuildingModelShapeSchema`, not solver validation or scoring. `core.ts:levelsSiteAudit`, `siteContainment`, `spacesAudit`, `wallsAudit`, and `slabsColumnsAudit` independently check polygon containment, including physical orthogonal wall solids. Its geometry module reconstructs evidence independently. Setback distances are rectangular-only; nonrectangular setbacks are NOT_EVALUATED. `openings.ts`, `stairs.ts`, and `circulation.ts` independently reconstruct host topology, landings/voids and an entry-rooted graph. Entry IDs are caller-supplied. QA is authoritative in `apps/api/src/services/project-service.ts:saveProjectRevision` before persistence; solver scores cannot bypass it.

`apps/api/src/lib/generation-identity.ts:requirementsHash` hashes normalized native requirements and engine version, excluding seed. `modelHash` hashes canonically ordered model collections excluding provenance. Execution and revision IDs remain distinct. `saveProjectRevision` records engine provenance; immutable JSONB revision persistence needs no migration for optional model extensions. Historical V1 normalization must remain available for its requirements hashes and stored revisions.

`apps/api/src/ai/architectural-interpreter.ts` produces V1-compatible rectangular requirements. Parking, daylight and ventilation are explicitly deferred; orientation is unsupported. No privacy objective exists yet. Its output must only claim applied intent once V2 consumes it. `apps/api/src/contracts/projects.ts` currently validates both requests and stored normalized requirements with the V1 schema, so migration must accept historical and V2 requirements separately.

Phase 13–15 consume canonical models and typed coverage. Parking must be a site entity with independent QA and explicit unevaluated material coverage, never an indoor room or an implied zero-price material. Phase 16 capability metadata must report the new active version without changing historical revision provenance.

## Planned bounded scope

Retain V1 entry points, normalization and fixtures. V2 will explore exact inscribed rectangular envelopes from an orthogonal coordinate-cell decomposition, with graph-informed floor/room/bank candidates and explicit evaluation. This supports a building within an L-shaped site without occupying its notch; multi-wing building envelopes are a separate limitation. Keep one/two storeys, exact existing construction primitives and independent QA. Add explicit access semantics, optional conceptual parking and scored geometric potential. Do not infer geographic front from north, simulate daylight/airflow, or claim code compliance.
